**Lesson:** changing stage is a disciplined search decision,not a promise of
improvement. Faster price eligibility failed in this unchanged-gate SPY context;
keep its negative evidence and avoid optimizing EMA length to rescue it.
Next uncertainty is attribution from saved decisions/ledgers—extra entries,
shortened profitable holdings and missed rebounds—before another financial cycle.
This does not reject all price-based strategies or establish financial alpha.
