## Accounting clarification before any historical launch

The newly implemented native USD account credits each distribution entitlement at
verified payment date, rounded half-even to cents per action. Exact unpaid
entitlements remain in NAV; the cent difference on payment is explicit rounding
P&L. Receivables cannot finance orders. Quantities reflect prior-close eligible
holdings, including after they sell on ex-date. A payment on a non-session date is
made spendable at the next simulated session; no intervening trading is modeled.
Final unpaid entitlements remain marked receivables, never future cash credited early.
These conventions apply identically to all five rules; taxes/withholding remain excluded.

Official issuer distributions are the primary source for a new qualified action
file. Provider precision differences and the2021-12-17SPY disagreement are preserved
in the source-join receipt; the old snapshot and old SPY results are not rewritten.
The issuer amount is1.636431USD/share versus1.633 in the saved provider snapshot;
the cause of this discrepancy is unverified. Any adoption is explicit in a new
input bundle and all controls are recomputed under the same source policy.
The total-return signal starts at the first observed closing mark; a distribution
on that anchor date does not imply a return before the anchor. Its original action
record remains intact. This clarification precedes financial outcomes and does
not change strategy thresholds, lookbacks, instruments or the comparison criterion.
