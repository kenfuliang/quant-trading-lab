Model, metrics and limitations
  • EMA uses only the previous completed close. Buy/hold means an always-long signal with daily resizing. Whole shares; $1 per trade plus 5 bps per side. Dividend cash is redeployed through daily sizing.
  • Debit interest includes weekends and is charged before the next open, with no positive-cash interest. Fixed 6%/9% are scenarios for every year, not historical broker quotes.
  • Margin checks at open before trading, after trading, daily low and close. A breach liquidates all at the checked price less costs and halts re-entry for the remaining sample. Daily-low liquidation is a conservative bar-based convention, not a guaranteed fill.
  • Overall Calmar = CAGR / |full-period daily-close drawdown|. Annual Calmar = calendar return / |within-year daily-close drawdown|. Annual peaks reset at prior year-end equity. Sharpe uses sample daily stdev, √252 and 0% risk-free; synthetic starting anchor excluded.
  • Undefined scores are N/A; insolvent paths stay recorded without flooring liabilities to zero. Exposure and margin buffers use checked marks. Broker SMA, settlement, changing house requirements, partial fills, taxes and intraday execution availability are omitted.
  • All eight base paths and both stress sets have independently reconciled trade and financing ledgers. No options, futures, broker connection or live orders.
