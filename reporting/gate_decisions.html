Definitions and interpretation limits
  • Helped during base loss: actionable lower target, negative subsequent base return and higher gate return.
  • Missed rebound: actionable lower target, negative raw-SPY return over the prior 21 sessions, then positive base return that exceeds the gate return. No hindsight trough is used.
  • Warning already cash: no immediate target reduction or protection credit; future policies can still diverge.
  • Targets and inputs are reconstructed from immutable saved artifacts, not contemporaneously captured live logs. Dates imply a modeled close-to-next-open boundary; historical publication time is unverified.
  • Later decisions and compounding remain in each account path. This does not isolate the causal effect of one warning.
  • Combined gate includes price-peak conditions. Ablated gate removes all those conditions from entry and recovery; remaining state memory stays fixed.
  • Viewed development history, not independent alpha, calibrated prediction, trained RL, a loss guarantee or permission to trade.
