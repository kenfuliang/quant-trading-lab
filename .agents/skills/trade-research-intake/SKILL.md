# External trading research intake

Locate the repository and read [research intake](../../../docs/research-intake.md)
and [investment mandate](../../../docs/goals.md). For a durable research record,
use the [intake template](../../../docs/templates/research-intake.md); read
[documentation conventions](../../../docs/README.md) before writing it.

Follow the intake SOP to a screened/rejected/blocked outcome with an evidence-backed
next action. Verify current primary sources when researching online. Separate the
original claim from our own results; identify exact revision, data availability,
rights, costs, market/capital fit and missing assumptions. Mark unknowns explicitly.

Repository discovery does not authorize running downloaded code, adding packages,
strategy tuning, or broker operations. If reproduction is requested, scope its
inputs, environment and acceptance first under the development workflow. A claim
is not reproduced because a README reports it, and paper operation is not evidence
of profitability.
